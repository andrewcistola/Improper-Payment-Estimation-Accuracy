# Literature Review

A standalone narrative review, written to be submitted on its own or alongside the companion Monte Carlo manuscript in [`manuscripts/`](../manuscripts/). Both papers ask the same question: what is the comparative accuracy of the available methods for estimating improper payments in federal health programs?

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`improper-payment-estimation-accuracy-literature-review.md`](improper-payment-estimation-accuracy-literature-review.md) | *Methods for Estimating Improper Payments in Federal Health Programs: A Narrative Review of Their Comparative Accuracy.* Abstract, introduction, review approach, regulatory context, terminology and estimators (including plug-in and parametric-bootstrap audit-finding simulation), how each program measures improper payments (Part C and RADV, Part D, PERM, EIPM, CERT), evidence on comparative accuracy, fit by program, research agenda (with a summary of what the companion study's first stage found), conclusions, and 95 annotated references. Also rendered as [PDF](improper-payment-estimation-accuracy-literature-review.pdf), [Word](improper-payment-estimation-accuracy-literature-review.docx) and HTML |
| `figures/` | Figure used in the review |

Rebuild the PDF, Word and HTML versions with `python manuscripts/scripts/render_review.py` (or `manuscripts/scripts/render.sh`, which renders everything).

Before submission, complete the affiliation (in brackets) and check unlinked classic references against the originals.
